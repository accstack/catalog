# Audio sample clips: sources, licences, attribution

25 short excerpts (about 5 to 12 seconds each), one per language, so that readers of the catalog can hear the language. Every clip comes from an openly licensed collection and is redistributed here under that licence with attribution; no clip is taken from a source that forbids redistribution (recordings on Bible.is, Global Recordings Network and access-controlled archives are linked from the catalog, not copied). Excerpts are unmodified apart from trimming, mono downmix and loudness normalisation. To have a clip removed, open an issue on this repository.
