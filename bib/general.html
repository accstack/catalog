General works on stress and tone

Works about word stress, accent or tone that name no single language: theory, typology, surveys and cross-linguistic studies, found by enumerating the world's linguistics journals. None has been examined yet; they are listed so that a reader can find them. Works on a particular language are on that language's page of the bibliography.
